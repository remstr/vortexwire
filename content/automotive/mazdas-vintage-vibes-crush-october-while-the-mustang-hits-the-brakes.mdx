October’s sales reports are out, and it looks like the "aging" kids on the block are having the last laugh. Mazda just flexed its way to its second-best October on record, proving that a solid lineup beats a flashy rebrand any day.

The real shocker? The veteran Mazda3 sedan saw sales skyrocket by over 183%. Apparently, being one of the oldest compacts on the market is the new "vintage cool," as buyers ignore the lack of tactile buttons for a reliable ride.

- Mazda’s Win: Total sales jumped 25%, led by the workhorse CX-5 crossover.
- Mustang’s Slump: Ford’s pony car hit a pothole, with sales sliding nearly 20% this month.
- EV Pulse: The Kia EV6 and Hyundai Ioniq 5 are showing signs of life, despite the EV market generally feeling like a ghost town since tax credits evaporated.

Ford is struggling to keep the Mustang’s momentum, selling just 3,092 units in October—a far cry from the six-figure glory days a decade ago. It seems the roar of a V8 is losing out to the practical hum of a hybrid as gas prices remain stubbornly high.

While the rugged Mazda CX-50 took a minor dip, the brand is still on track for a massive year. It turns out Americans want crossovers, hybrids, and—against all odds—a compact Mazda sedan that’s older than some TikTok trends.
