BMW isn’t just winning the luxury sales war; they’re effectively lapping the competition. Since 2021, the Bavarians have kept Mercedes-Benz in the rearview mirror, and their 2027 roadmap suggests they have no intention of slowing down.

The headliner is the "i1," a new entry-level EV that finally gets the memo on brand heritage. Reports say it’s ditching front-wheel drive to return to a proper rear-wheel-drive setup, serving as a compact electric scalpel for the purists.

Things get a little chaotic with the crossovers. While the gas-powered X1 gets a standard mid-cycle refresh, the electric iX1 is skipping the facelift entirely to jump straight into a new generation on a dedicated EV platform.

- The i1: A 2028 launch with a 2027 reveal. It brings RWD back to the entry-level tier.
- Next-gen iX1: Moving to cylindrical batteries for a massive range boost and a minimalist, "button-allergic" cabin.
- Global Dominance: This "Neue Klasse" offensive aims to keep BMW’s crown glued to its head.

Whether these smaller models actually land in U.S. showrooms is still a coin toss. However, with sleek designs and high-voltage performance on the horizon, Munich is clearly playing for keeps. Even if you miss the tactile click of a physical volume knob, it’s hard to argue with the momentum.
