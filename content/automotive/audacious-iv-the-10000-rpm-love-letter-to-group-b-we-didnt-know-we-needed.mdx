UK-based Audacious Automotive is currently proving that if you throw enough money and engineering at a nostalgia trip, actual magic happens. The Audacious IV is a Frankenstein’s monster of German engineering, blending the iconic box-flared soul of the original Ur-Quattro with the high-revving guts of a B7-generation RS4.

This isn’t just a cosmetic kit; it’s a surgical transplant that keeps the RS4’s chassis, manual gearbox, and AWD system while wrapping it in a stunning carbon-fiber shell. While the base tune delivers a healthy 592 hp, the real headline is the development of a 10,000-rpm V8 variant that likely sounds like a symphony for the speed-obsessed.

- **The Stats:** 0-60 in 3.4 seconds and a top speed of 200 mph.
- **The Power:** Choose between the standard 592 hp or a supercharged 740 hp beast.
- **The Exclusivity:** Only 50 units will exist, with 40 getting the full carbon fiber treatment.

Of course, playing God with Audi history isn’t cheap. You’ll need to hand over roughly $468,000 and provide both donor vehicles—an original Quattro and an RS4—just to get started. 

Waiting three years for delivery might feel like an eternity, but for a car that looks like a vintage rally stage and screams like a late-night street race, it’s a small price to pay. Just don’t tell your accountant where the money went.
