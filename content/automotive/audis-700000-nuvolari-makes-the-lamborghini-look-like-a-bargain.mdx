Audi is finally ditching its "sensible sedan" reputation for something significantly more aggressive and much more expensive. Meet the Nuvolari, a 987-horsepower hypercar that makes a $400,000 Lamborghini look like a sensible entry-level purchase.

The German automaker is capping production at 499 units globally, and only 182 of those are destined for American soil. If you haven't already cleared space in your climate-controlled garage, you’re likely already too late to the party.

- **The Muscle:** A twin-turbo V8 plus three electric motors produces enough torque to rearrange your internal organs.
- **The Physics:** Active aerodynamics generate 880 pounds of downforce, keeping the car glued to the tarmac at its 217 mph top speed.
- **The Style:** It debuts Audi’s new "restrained" design language, though there’s nothing restrained about a $700,000 sticker price.

The customization options are bordering on the absurd, with 33 leather choices and an optional fully exposed carbon-fiber exterior. You can even choose between 13 different seatbelt colors, because why should your safety equipment clash with your loafers?

Order books are open for a "limited time," with the first deliveries scheduled for the first half of 2028. Just don't forget to budget for the destination charge—because apparently, $700,000 doesn't cover the delivery fee.
