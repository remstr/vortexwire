Maserati has spent the last few years looking like that one luxury brand everyone forgets is still at the party. Sales of the Levante SUV were so sluggish that production finally flatlined earlier this year, and the Quattroporte bowed out in 2023 without much of a standing ovation. 

But on December 10, the Trident is reportedly heading back to Modena to show the world they still have a pulse. According to *The Supercar Blog*, we’re getting two concepts that define the brand’s new design direction. 

One is a new Levante, which needs to be significantly more interesting than its predecessor if it wants to steal a single Porsche Cayenne owner’s lunch money. The real curveball, however, is the Quattroporte. 

CEO Santo Ficili recently admitted he doesn't think the next flagship should be a sedan. Instead, he’s dreaming of a "very low, big car" that sits somewhere between a shooting brake and a crossover. 

- **The Reveal:** December 10 in Modena, Italy.
- **The Levante:** A return to the E-segment SUV space with a heavy design pivot.
- **The Quattroporte:** A "low and big" hybrid body style that refuses to be a traditional four-door.

Whether this actually saves the brand or just adds more confusion to the showroom remains to be seen. But hey, at least they’re trying something other than another generic SUV.
