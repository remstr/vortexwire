Michael Schumacher spent the early 2000s winning everything in sight for Ferrari. But apparently, even the Red Baron needed a break from Maranello, leading to a secret, high-speed relationship with this 2004 Porsche Carrera GT.

Because Ferrari contracts aren't big on "endorsing the competition," the car was titled to his longtime manager, Willi Weber. The move kept the paper trail clean while Schumacher spent his downtime wrangling 605 horses and a raw six-speed manual.

- Chassis 000116: A black-on-black masterpiece and one of only 1,270 ever built.
- The Goods: A naturally aspirated 5.7-liter V10 that screams its way to a 205-mph top speed.
- The Evidence: Documentation includes photos of Schumi behind the wheel and a cheeky "MS" vinyl insignia on the rear decklid.

This pristine specimen hits the block at Mecum’s Kissimmee auction on January 16, 2027. It’s essentially the ultimate "don't tell my boss" trophy for whichever collector has the deepest pockets in Florida.

The Carrera GT is already a holy grail thanks to its Le Mans-derived engine and a reputation for being delightfully terrifying to drive. Adding Schumacher’s personal history to the provenance makes it a one-of-one automotive flex.

If you want the car that proved the F1 GOAT had a wandering eye for Stuttgart engineering, start checking your couch cushions now. Just don’t mention it to the folks in Maranello—they’re probably still a little sensitive.
