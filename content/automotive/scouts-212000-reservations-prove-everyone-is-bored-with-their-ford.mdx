Scout Motors is staging a massive comeback, and it seems half of the country is ready to trade in their Bronco for a taste of electrified nostalgia. With 212,000 reservations and counting for the upcoming Traveler SUV and Terra pickup, the VW-backed startup is already haunting Detroit’s boardrooms before a single production model has even hit the dirt.

Data from a recent media briefing in South Carolina shows the brand isn’t just attracting nostalgic gearheads; it’s actively poaching from the biggest players in the 4x4 world. It turns out that people who spend their weekends in the mud are very ready for something new that doesn't involve a blue oval or a seven-slot grille.

- Ford Bronco and Jeep Wrangler owners are the most likely to ditch their current keys for the Traveler SUV.
- F-150 drivers are leading the charge for the Terra pickup, followed closely by Tesla and Rivian fans looking for more "old school" grit.
- Even the Toyota Tacoma and Ford Maverick crowds are making appearances on the waitlist, proving nobody is safe.

This isn’t just a niche play for enthusiasts. Tesla Model 3 and Model Y owners are cracking the top ten lists, suggesting the EV crowd is finally tired of looking like every other anonymous crossover in the Whole Foods parking lot. 

Granted, a $100 reservation isn’t a blood oath, and full production won't truly ramp up until 2028. But Scout has already managed to build a massive, diverse community that’s ready to trade status symbols for some rugged, electric utility.
