Most people prep their car for a trade-in by clearing out the glovebox and hitting the local car wash. One driver in Tempe, Arizona, opted for a more felony-adjacent farewell, allegedly pushing his 2023 BMW M2 to a terminal 170 mph.

Arizona State Troopers spotted the gray blur weaving through traffic on State Route 202 near Sky Harbor Boulevard. Using LIDAR, they confirmed the car was traveling 105 mph over the 65-mph limit—a speed that covers nearly 250 feet every second.

- **The Logic:** The driver told police he was trading the car in and wanted one final blast before handing over the keys.
- **The Disconnect:** When first pulled over, he estimated he was only doing "110 to 115 mph," which is still an express ticket to a jail cell in most zip codes.
- **The Bonus:** To add flavor to the chaos, the BMW was missing a license plate when troopers finally cornered it off the McClintock Drive exit.

The Highway Patrol wisely chose not to engage in a high-speed pursuit, instead coordinating with traffic operations to pin the driver down after he exited the highway. It turns out "goodbye" is a lot more expensive when it includes arrests for reckless driving and endangerment.

If you’re feeling sentimental about your lease ending, try a commemorative photo. Treating a public highway like the Mulsanne Straight is a great way to ensure your next ride is in the back of a police cruiser.
