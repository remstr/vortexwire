Buying a charred 2025 Corvette Z06 for $70,000 sounds like an expensive way to buy a giant, soot-covered paperweight. YouTuber Alex Palmeri did exactly that, gambling on a top-trim 3LZ model that looked like it had been through a backyard grill session.

The "fire damage" was only half the story. Since the targa top was missing while it sat in the auction lot, the interior was less "new car smell" and more "stagnant pond."

- **The Damage:** A salvage title, 3,000 miles, and carbon fiber trim that had yellowed under the intense heat.
- **The Restoration:** Professional detailers pulled literal gallons of brown soot-water out of the high-end suede competition seats.
- **The Fix:** Surprisingly, about $300 in secondhand dash parts and a diagnostic reset were the only things standing between a wreck and a runner.

The real headache wasn't the melted paint-protection film; it was the total silence from the engine. The fuel lines were bone dry, and the dashboard refused to communicate with the rest of the car.

It turns out the Z06 wasn't mechanically dead; it was just having a digital panic attack. After being tossed around by aggressive auction forklifts, the car’s safety logic assumed it had rolled over and cut the fuel delivery entirely.

One scan tool reset later, the flat-plane crank V8 finally screamed back to life. It’s still a work-in-progress, but for $70k, Palmeri’s toasted gamble is officially a rolling success.
