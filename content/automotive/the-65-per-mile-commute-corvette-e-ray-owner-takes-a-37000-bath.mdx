Most people expect to lose a little scratch when they drive a new car off the lot. One Corvette E-Ray owner just lost the equivalent of a brand-new base model Camaro before even finishing his first three tanks of gas.

This 2025 3LZ Convertible recently hit the digital auction block on Cars & Bids with a mere 570 miles on the odometer. Originally purchased for a hefty $136,890, the hammer finally dropped at a sobering $99,500. 

That is a nearly 30% depreciation hit for a vehicle that hasn’t even reached its first oil change. At roughly $65 per mile, the seller effectively paid for a five-star steak dinner every time he dared to back out of the driveway.

While the E-Ray is a technical beast—offering 655 horsepower and a blistering 2.5-second 0-60 sprint—the market seems lukewarm on electrified 'Vettes. Traditionalists are currently holding their breath for the high-octane ZR1, leaving this hybrid middle child in a temporary pricing basement.

- Original MSRP: $136,890
- Auction Result: $99,500
- The "Ouch" Factor: A $37,390 loss in record time.

The seller doesn't seem particularly bothered by the financial bruising, noting he has a garage full of other toys to keep him occupied. Meanwhile, the lucky buyer just snagged the performance bargain of the year on a supercar that still has the factory plastic on the floorboards.
