Mattel just gave your inner child—and your dwindling bank account—a reason to celebrate. The new Hot Wheels Brick Shop Racer Series is launching in January, shrinking the building-block experience down to the classic 1:64 scale we’ve all been stepping on for decades.

At a mere $4.99, these aren’t the wallet-draining kits that require a PhD and a three-day weekend to assemble. Each set features roughly 28 pieces, blending the click-and-snap of bricks with the heavy-duty metal baseplates and rolling axles that made Hot Wheels a household staple.

- The Garage: The initial lineup features a 2024 Chevy Camaro ZL1, 2018 Audi TT RS, Maserati MCPura, 1991 Acura NSX, 1999 Honda S2000, and the legendary Bone Shaker.
- The Roadmap: Expect six cars this winter, with the collection ballooning to 20 models by the end of next year.

By keeping things small and affordable, Mattel is parking itself directly on Lego’s lawn. It’s a strategic play for parents tired of toy-aisle inflation and collectors who want the satisfaction of a build without needing a dedicated storage unit for the finished product.

The assembly is simple enough for a six-year-old, but the nostalgia bait is clearly aimed at the rest of us. Your desk clutter is about to get a lot more customizable.
