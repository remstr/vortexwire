SEMA is the place where "extra" is the baseline, but Ringbrothers just reset the curve. They poured 5,500 hours—nearly seven months of non-stop labor—into transforming a humble Pontiac Tempest into “Billie,” a 1965 GTO that’s five inches wider and infinitely meaner.

Widening a classic without making it look like a botched plastic surgery job is a delicate art. The team massaged every factory line and lengthened the rockers to ensure the proportions remained iconic, hiding massive fabrication under a custom “Billie Goat Blue” finish.

- **The Muscle:** A supercharged 6.2-liter LT4 V8 delivering 650 horsepower and 650 lb-ft of torque via a 10-speed automatic.
- **The Tech:** It rides on a Fast Track Stage III chassis with Fox RS SV coilovers and Brembo six-piston stoppers.
- **The Diet:** Extensive carbon fiber usage on the hood, valance, and rear diffuser keeps the weight in check.

The interior didn't escape the knife either, with the dashboard repositioned four inches forward to create a more ergonomic cockpit. It’s packed with Dakota Digital gauges and a restored factory radio that hides modern Bluetooth connectivity for your road trip playlists.

It’s the kind of build that makes you realize your "project car" in the garage is just a pile of parts. If you aren't spending thousands of hours on custom aluminum taillight panels, are you even trying?
