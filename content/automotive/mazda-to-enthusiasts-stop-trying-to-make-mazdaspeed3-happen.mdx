If you’ve been holding a candle for the return of the Mazdaspeed3, it’s time to blow it out. Mazda just poured a very large, very cold bucket of water on the latest reports suggesting a 295-horsepower performance hatch was in the works.

Vinesh Bhindi, Mazda Australia’s managing director, recently confirmed that the rumors are essentially automotive fan fiction. There is no secret, tire-shredding project lurking in the Hiroshima basement.

- Mazda3 sales exploded by 145.5% this past October.
- The brand has moved nearly 50% more units so far this year than last.

The original Speed3 was a glorious, torque-steering menace that burned bright from 2007 to 2013. However, modern Mazda has traded that rowdy energy for its "mature" Kodo design era, focusing on premium interiors rather than hunting Golf GTIs.

The consolation prize isn't exactly a slow-mobile. The top-tier current Mazda3 offers a turbo-four with 320 lb-ft of torque, which is only 13 horsepower shy of the legendary predecessor.

- You can still row your own gears with a six-speed manual.
- All-wheel drive remains an option for those who actually like grip.

Ultimately, Mazda is making too much money on the standard model to care about niche performance projects. The Speed3 badge is staying in the vault, but at least the manual transmission isn't dead yet.
