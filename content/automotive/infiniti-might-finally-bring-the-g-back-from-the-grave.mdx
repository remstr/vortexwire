Infiniti spent the last decade trying to make us care about the "Q" nomenclature, but nobody ever fell in love with a letter that sounds like a confusing FAQ page. Now, the brand is hinting at a return to form that actually has enthusiasts looking up from their phones.

At the LA Auto Show, Infiniti’s product boss Ponz Pandikuthira teased that the legendary "G" badge could return for their upcoming performance sedan. We’re talking about a legitimate successor to the G35 and G37—cars people actually liked before the brand went through its weirdly corporate mid-life crisis.

- Heart of the beast: A 400-horsepower twin-turbo V6 borrowed from the Nissan Z.
- The real hero: A six-speed manual transmission is expected to be on the menu.
- Heritage: It’s essentially a tuxedo-wearing version of the next-gen Nissan Skyline.

The outgoing Q50 Red Sport had the muscle, but it felt about as emotional as a high-end microwave. This new model aims to reclaim the "G" glory days by catering to the buyers who have been haunting dealerships asking for a modern G37 since 2014.

While the name isn't 100% locked in yet, Pandikuthira admitted that calling it a "G" wouldn't be off the mark. Expect this three-pedal luxury sleeper to hit US showrooms next year, finally giving us a reason to visit an Infiniti dealership that isn’t just for a lease return.
