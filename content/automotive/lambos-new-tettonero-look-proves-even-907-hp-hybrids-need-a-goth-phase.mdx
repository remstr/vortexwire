Lamborghini just dropped the "Tettonero" edition of its Temerario hybrid, and it’s essentially the automotive equivalent of a perfectly tailored tuxedo. Developed by the geniuses at Lamborghini Centro Stile, this two-tone treatment pairs a Nero Noctis hood and roof with whatever highlighter-bright body color makes your neighbors the most jealous.

The name "Tettonero" isn’t exactly new—it first showed up on the Urus SE—but now the brand's plug-in baby supercar gets to join the dark-roof club. It’s a purely aesthetic flex, meaning there are no extra horses under the hood, but when you’re already pushing 907 hp, do you really need more?

- Engine: 4.0-liter twin-turbo V8 paired with three electric motors.
- Power: 907 hp (unchanged, because it's already a rocket).
- Aesthetic: Two-tone "Nero Noctis" upper surfaces and gloss black exterior accents.
- Interior: Nero Ade upholstery with enough contrast stitching to make a master tailor weep.

Don’t expect a fancy limited-edition plaque on the dashboard. This isn't a "numbered series" car; it’s a way for Lamborghini to showcase its Ad Personam customization while keeping the Temerario’s momentum going after its splashy Pebble Beach debut.

The car retains the signature hexagonal seat quilting, because if there’s one thing Lambo loves more than speed, it’s geometry. Whether you’re waiting for the rumored off-roader or the inevitable spyder, the Tettonero proves that looking fast is at least half the battle.
