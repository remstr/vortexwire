Ferrari just dropped the 296 Challenge Stradale, a $307,000 love letter to internal combustion that ditches hybrid batteries for 670 horses of pure, unadulterated scream. It’s essentially a track car with a license plate, designed for people who think "excess" is just a starting point.

The real sport, however, isn't on the tarmac—it’s in the online configurator. If you have thirty minutes to kill and a looming sense of financial irresponsibility, this is your new favorite time-sink.

You start with 35 paint options, including "Giallo Ambra," which is Italian for "I want my supercar to look like a very fast, very expensive tangerine." From there, the options get delightfully obsessive.

Ferrari lets you swap out almost every body panel for carbon fiber and replace the glass with Lexan to shave off every possible ounce. You can even ditch the physical Prancing Horse badges for painted ones, because apparently, a few grams of metal is the only thing standing between you and a podium finish.

- $307,000 base price before you start clicking the "make it carbon" button.
- A non-hybrid V6 powertrain that proves Ferrari hasn’t gone completely quiet just yet.
- Interior choices include 26 stitching colors and "Best Weight" seats that likely offer the comfort of a very fast park bench.

Whether you’re opting for titanium wheel bolts or orange stitching to match your exterior, the 296 CS is a masterclass in personalized vanity. It’s ridiculous, overpriced, and exactly why we love cars that make no sense.
