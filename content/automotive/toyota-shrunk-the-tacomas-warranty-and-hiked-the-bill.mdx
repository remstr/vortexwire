Toyota is testing the limits of brand loyalty with the 2027 Tacoma, offering a classic "pay more, get less" deal. The base SR price has climbed to $34,470 once you fold in those mandatory delivery fees, while the top-shelf TRD Pro Hybrid now commands a staggering $66,675.

The real kicker isn’t just the sticker shock—it’s the gutting of the ToyotaCare program. What used to be two years or 25,000 miles of free scheduled maintenance has been slashed to just one year or 10,000 miles. 

- Base price: Starts at $34,470 for the SR.
- TRD Pro Hybrid: Tops out at $66,675.
- Maintenance: Cut from two years down to one.

Toyota claims this reduction was driven by “customer feedback.” Apparently, Tacoma owners were dying to start paying for their own oil changes a year earlier—a level of corporate gaslighting that deserves its own trophy.

It’s not all belt-tightening and service bills, though. The mid-range SR5 trim now includes heated, powered front seats and dark-silver wheels as standard equipment, features that previously required a pricey upgrade.

- New "Honeycomb" yellow paint is exclusive to the TRD Pro.
- i-Force Max hybrid engine still pumps out 326 horsepower.
- Roadside assistance remains at two years with unlimited miles.

If you’re weighing a 2027 model against a leftover 2026, the math is getting ugly. You’re essentially paying a premium for the privilege of visiting the service department on your own dime 12 months sooner.
