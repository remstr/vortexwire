Jeep usually saves its coolest toys for the military, and the Wrangler Commando 392 is no exception. Of the 250 units produced, 249 were reserved exclusively for active service members and veterans, leaving the rest of us to stare longingly from the civilian sidelines.

But Jeep just broke its own "no civilians allowed" rule for one final, wildly over-the-top build. This Army Green unicorn is currently live on Bring a Trailer, with every single cent—including the buyer’s fee—heading straight to the USO to support military families.

- **The Power:** A 3.0-liter Whipple supercharger turns the factory 470-hp V8 into a 705-hp monster.
- **The Gear:** It’s sitting on a 3.5-inch JKS lift, Fox 3.0 internal bypass shocks, and 37-inch Nitto tires on beadlock-capable wheels.
- **The Look:** Unlike the camo-wrapped military units, this one-off rocks a clean "Commando" graphic and bronze wheels.

There is no reserve price, meaning someone is walking away with this supercharged brick on November 22, regardless of where the bidding stops. It’s essentially a high-performance tank that funds programs for troops, which is a hell of a lot better than your standard tax-deductible donation.

If you were one of the 4,700 people who applied for the original run and got rejected, here is your high-octane consolation prize. Just bring a heavy wallet and a very heavy right foot.
