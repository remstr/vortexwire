Toyota finally realized 4Runner owners don’t actually enjoy frostbite, but that comfort comes with a steeper bill. The 2027 entry-level SR5 just vaulted over the $45,000 mark for the first time.

That $45,085 sticker—a $1,220 jump from last year—includes standard heated, eight-way power-adjustable front seats. It’s a solid consolation prize for a base trim that used to treat basic warmth like a premium luxury.

- Every 2027 trim now includes heated seats and the Toyota Safety Sense 3.0 suite.
- The TRD Sport and TRD Off-Road trims saw an $870 price hike for the new year.
- Hybrid models now start at $55,945, offering a beefier 326-horsepower powertrain.

Most other trims saw more modest $200 bumps, though the top-tier Platinum now projects the 4Runner logo onto the pavement when you open the door. Because nothing says "I spent sixty grand" like a vanity light for the sidewalk.

If you’re feeling spicy, the TRD Pro offers a new "Hot Honey" paint job for an extra $695. For everyone else, the color "Black" has been rebranded as "Inked," which sounds more like a lifestyle choice than a paint code.

Expect these rigs to hit dealerships toward the end of the year. Just make sure your bank account is as rugged as the suspension.
