Scion is crawling out of the automotive graveyard, but it isn't bringing another boxy hatchback to the party. Toyota is officially reviving the badge as "Scion by Toyota" to launch the KEI-01, a pint-sized pickup designed specifically for US soil. 

For years, every car enthusiast on the internet has sworn they’d buy a Japanese Kei truck if it were actually legal here. Toyota is finally calling that bluff, though the reality is a bit more "neighborhood watch" than "highway hauler."

- The KEI-01 features a 660-cc three-cylinder engine paired with 4WD and a CVT.
- Its rear bed sides fold down, making it easier to load up to 600 pounds of whatever you can fit in five feet of space.

Before you start planning a cross-country road trip, there is a significant asterisk attached to your tiny-truck fantasies. To satisfy Low-Speed Vehicle laws, the KEI-01 is electronically limited to a top speed of just 25 mph. 

It is essentially a street-legal, ruggedized golf cart with an interior that actually offers heat, A/C, and a touchscreen. Initial sales are restricted to fleet customers, though Toyota says they’ll consider a retail release if enough people scream for it.

The truck is joined by two other concepts: a 300-hp hybrid side-by-side and a rugged electric scooter. Expect the KEI-01 to hit the pavement—slowly—in late 2027 with a price tag hovering near $20,000.
