Hyundai Australia just pulled the plug on the Sonata after a sales performance so grim it borders on a comedy of errors. In October 2026, the company moved exactly eight units—not eight thousand, just eight individual cars. 

To put that in perspective, you could fit every Australian Sonata buyer from last month into two slightly crowded elevators. Meanwhile, the Toyota Camry is still doing victory laps Down Under with over 10,000 sales in the same period.

- Sonata production for the Australian market officially ended in October.
- The gap will be filled by a bloated, "next-gen" i30 (Elantra) that’s basically a mid-size sedan in a trench coat.
- This new i30 arrives in 2027 as a hybrid-only affair to appease the efficiency gods.

Before you start mourning the four-door stateside, relax. Hyundai Motor North America CEO Randy Parker reaffirmed at the LA Auto Show that Americans aren't ready to surrender to the SUV overlords just yet. 

While the rest of the world treats sedans like flip phones, the US market snapped up 1.7 million of them in 2025. Sedans still account for roughly 13% of industry sales here, proving that some of us still prefer a trunk to a liftgate.

The Sonata might be getting the boot in the Outback, but in the land of the free, it’s still staying on the playlist for another season.
