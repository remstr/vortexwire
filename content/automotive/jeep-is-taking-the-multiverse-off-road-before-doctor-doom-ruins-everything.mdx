Jeep is officially bored with Earth’s terrain. After eight decades of conquering deserts and suburban mall parking lots, the brand is spending the rest of the year hopping through the Marvel multiverse.

The final chapter of a year-long "Avengers: Doomsday" collab just dropped, and it’s a glorious fever dream of corporate synergy. We’re talking about Jeeps drifting through the X-Mansion and rolling straight into Doctor Doom’s Latverian fortress.

The visual highlight is undoubtedly Ben Grimm—better known as The Thing—squeezing his massive, rocky frame behind the wheel of a Wrangler. If the suspension can handle a pilot made of orange boulders, your pothole-ridden commute should be a breeze.

- Captain America and Nightcrawler make cameos, while H.E.R.B.I.E. the robot likely handles the tech specs.
- The campaign celebrates a shared 1941 "birth year" between the Jeep brand and Steve Rogers.
- Unlike previous phases, this push features the entire portfolio rather than a single special edition truck.

This isn't just a gimmick to sell more Gladiators. Jeep is leaning hard into its "go anywhere" ethos, even if "anywhere" happens to be an alternate dimension where a guy in a metal mask is trying to rewrite reality.

Catch the creative across social and TV before *Avengers: Doomsday* hits theaters on December 18. Just don’t expect your local dealer to offer a warranty that covers portal-related wear and tear.
