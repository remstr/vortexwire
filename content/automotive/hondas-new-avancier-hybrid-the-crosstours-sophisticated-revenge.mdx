Honda is digging through its archives, dusting off the Avancier nameplate for a three-row hybrid that refuses to pick a side in the SUV-versus-wagon war. The teaser reveals a sleek, rakish silhouette that looks more like a high-riding shooting brake than your neighbor’s boxy Pilot.

This isn't just a styling exercise; it’s the flagship for the brand's new "Honda Hybrid" branding. Honda is ditching the clunky "e:HEV" label in favor of something that doesn't require a decoder ring to understand.

The Avancier is the first of 15 next-gen hybrids hitting the streets by 2030. It rides on a fresh, lightweight platform that promises more rigidity and less bloat, potentially paired with a new electric AWD system.

- New "Honda Hybrid" tech: A simpler name for a more efficient, globally-shared platform.
- Three-row flexibility: The utility of a hauler with the aerodynamics of something you’d actually want to be seen in.
- Sharp design: L-shaped lighting and creased bodywork that marks a clean break from Honda's current rounder fleet.

For those still nursing emotional scars from the mid-2000s Accord Crosstour, breathe easy—this looks significantly more sophisticated. While an Australian launch is locked for 2027, the US remains a "maybe," though it would fit perfectly into Honda’s plan to phase out gas-only models. If it does cross the Pacific, expect an Accord badge to help it fight the established family-hauler hierarchy.
