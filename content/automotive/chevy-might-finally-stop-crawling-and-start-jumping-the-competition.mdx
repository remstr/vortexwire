GM’s current off-road strategy has essentially been "slow and steady wins the race." While the Silverado ZR2 and its GMC AT4X cousins are fantastic for rock crawling, they aren't exactly built for high-speed desert heroics.

Try to launch a current Silverado over a sand dune like an F-150 Raptor, and you’ll likely turn your expensive truck into a very shiny lawn ornament. But the tide is finally turning in Detroit.

Internal rumblings suggest GM is finally developing a proper apex predator to hunt the Raptor R and Ram TRX. It’s about time, honestly, since Chevrolet has been playing second fiddle in the sand for far too long.

- The Mission: Build a Silverado capable of high-speed "unplanned flight" without shedding its suspension components.
- The Market: People who want to look like they spend weekends in Baja, even if they’re actually just navigating the local Starbucks drive-thru.
- The Competition: Toyota is also entering the arena with the "TRD Hammer"—a name that sounds less like a pickup and more like a mid-tier 90s wrestling villain.

Chevy has the heritage to back this up, dating back to a legendary class-winning S-10 at the 1982 Baja 500. They have the torquey V8s and the Multimatic tech; they just need the guts to go fast.

If these rumors hold water, we might finally see a bowtie that can actually take flight. Let’s just hope GM focuses on the bypass shocks and skips the hilariously unfortunate naming conventions.
