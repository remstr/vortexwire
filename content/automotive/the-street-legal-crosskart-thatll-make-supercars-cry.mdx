Most people buy a project car to fix a leaky head gasket or feel a sense of accomplishment. YouTuber Vasiliy Builds decided to build a "Crosskart" that looks less like a vehicle and more like a roll cage with a serious vendetta.

This isn't your local amusement park’s putt-putt racer. It’s a tube-frame monster powered by a turbocharged Suzuki Hayabusa engine, aiming for a terrifying 300 horsepower.

With a projected weight under 1,000 pounds, this thing will have a power-to-weight ratio that makes most Lamborghinis look like overpriced golf carts. Vasiliy is also throwing in long-travel suspension for trophy-truck vibes and active aero to keep the whole mess from taking flight.

The best—or most unhinged—part of the build is that Vasiliy actually wants to license it for the road. Imagine pulling up to a Starbucks drive-thru in what is essentially a caffeinated skeleton on wheels.

- Turbocharged Hayabusa engine targeting a massive 300 hp.
- Long-travel suspension designed for trophy-truck levels of off-road abuse.
- Active aerodynamics to manage the insane power-to-weight ratio.
- A goal of full street legality for the ultimate, terrifying grocery getter.

The project is still in the "everything is difficult" phase as the builder tries to cram massive hardware into the tightest frame possible. If he pulls it off, he won't just have a hobby; he'll have a land-bound missile that turns every commute into a survival horror game.
