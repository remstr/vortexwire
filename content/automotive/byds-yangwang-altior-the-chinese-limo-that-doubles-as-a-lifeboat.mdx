BYD’s high-end Yangwang sub-brand just unveiled the Altior in Abu Dhabi, and it’s less of a car and more of a rolling sovereign nation. At over 220 inches long, this electric flagship is designed to make the Rolls-Royce Phantom look like a subcompact rental.

The design is inspired by the ancient Chinese "ding"—a ceremonial bronze vessel—which is fitting since you’ll need a literal pot of gold to park one in your driveway. The exterior features a "light-catching" grille and theatrical coach doors that swing wide enough to load a small piano.

- Quad-motor setup likely pushing north of 1,000 horsepower.
- Includes "tank turns" and an emergency flotation mode for when your GPS takes "turn right into the lake" literally.
- Bespoke umbrellas tucked into the doors, because stealing cues from the British is the ultimate luxury flex.

Inside, the Altior is a maximalist’s fever dream. We’re talking 39 speakers, two refrigerators, and enough jade and silk trim to make a museum curator sweat. 

The tech is equally extra. A camera-linked active suspension reads the pavement to keep your afternoon espresso perfectly still, while four independent motors adjust torque 100 times per second. 

BYD claims a 621-mile range, but that’s on the notoriously optimistic Chinese testing cycle. Expect significantly less mileage if this jade-encrusted land yacht ever reaches a US highway.
