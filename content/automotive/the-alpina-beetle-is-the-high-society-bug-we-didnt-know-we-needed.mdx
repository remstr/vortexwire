Alpina spent decades taking BMW’s best and turning them into sophisticated, leather-clad land missiles. But while the tuning house is now officially tucked under the BMW Group umbrella, digital artist Abimelec Design decided to ask the question nobody else dared: What if Alpina had a mid-life crisis and bought a Volkswagen?

Enter the "B1 Turbo," a fictional rendering that gives the New Beetle the full Buchloe treatment. It isn’t just a Bug with a body kit; it’s a masterclass in subtle German flex.

- The exterior sports that iconic deep blue paint and those signature multi-spoke wheels.
- Inside, the plastic-heavy VW cabin is swapped for a boutique, high-end interior inspired by the legendary Beetle RSi.

Abimelec’s vision stems from a ride in a real-world RSi in Mexico City, where the upscale feel of that rare widebody sparked a "what if" moment. This isn't his first rodeo with brand-blending, either, having previously Alpina-fied everything from Range Rovers to Porsche 911s.

Under the hood, this fictional beast packs a turbocharged VR6 engine. It’s the perfect powertrain for a car that looks like it belongs at a yacht club but drives like it’s escaping a heist.

Since Volkswagen killed the Beetle in 2019 and Alpina is busy with corporate integration, this remains a pixelated dream. Still, it’s a refreshing reminder that the automotive world could use a little more weirdness and a lot less beige.
