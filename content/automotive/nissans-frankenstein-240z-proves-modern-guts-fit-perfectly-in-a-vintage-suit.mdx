Nissan just dropped a restomod that makes your neighbor’s "project car" look like a half-finished Lego set. Built for SEMA 2026, the Datsun S30 Concept is a vintage 240Z that’s been surgically enhanced with the twin-turbo heart of the modern Z.

Working alongside drift king Chris Forsberg, Nissan didn't just slap on some stickers and call it a day. They shoved the VR30DDTT engine—upped with Garrett PMAX turbos—into the bay and hooked it to a proper six-speed manual.

- **Carbon Obsession:** Custom fenders, hood, hatch, and spoilers cut the weight without ruining the S30’s legendary 70s profile.
- **Track Ready:** A Radium 10-gallon fuel cell and DeatschWerks pump ensure it can handle high-boost abuse, not just idle on a convention floor.
- **The Guts:** Techno Toy Tuning coilovers and Wilwood six-piston brakes keep the 400-plus horses from ending up in a ditch.

The aesthetic is finished in a striking Seiran Blue, proving that while the tech is futuristic, the soul remains rooted in the disco era. It’s a functional laboratory on wheels, designed to show that the new Z’s DNA is more than just a marketing department fever dream.

If you want to see this Frankenstein's monster in person, it’ll be haunting the Las Vegas convention floor this November. Just don't expect to see it at a standstill for long.
