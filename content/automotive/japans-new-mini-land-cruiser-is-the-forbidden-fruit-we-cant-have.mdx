Toyota is currently playing a cruel game of “keep away” with the US market, and Japanese tuner Kuhl Racing is busy making sure it hurts. Their new “Iron Build” package takes the compact Land Cruiser FJ—a car we already can’t have—and turns it into a rugged little monster that puts the Ford Bronco Sport on notice.

This isn't just a cosmetic face-lift; it’s a full-blown attitude adjustment. Kuhl adds a heavy-duty suspension lift, chunky skid plates, and a steel front guard that says "I climb rocks" rather than "I do grocery runs."

Here is the gear that makes the build:
- 18-to-22-inch wheels paired with beefy Dunlop All-Terrains.
- Retro round headlights and a quad-exhaust setup for maximum curb appeal.
- An optional supercharger kit currently in development for those who find "stock" insulting.

The price tag is the real kicker. The full conversion costs roughly $6,500, bringing the total price for a modified, brand-new FJ to approximately $33,000. 

In a world where base-model crossovers are pushing $40k, a supercharged mini-tank for thirty-three grand feels like a fever dream. Unfortunately for us, it’s a dream that stays in Japan. While American enthusiasts settle for sensible daily drivers, Tokyo gets the off-road toys we’d actually sell a kidney for.
