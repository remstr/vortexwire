Ferrari is finally admitting that sometimes, less is actually a hell of a lot more. The new 296 Challenge Stradale just dropped, and it’s a high-octane love letter to purists who think batteries belong in TV remotes, not supercars.

By ditching the heavy hybrid system found in the standard 296, Maranello created a 670-horsepower beast that’s undergone a serious weight-loss program. It tips the scales at a lean 2,932 pounds, making it the closest thing to a professional race car you can legally park in a suburban driveway.

- Pure V6 Power: The twin-turbo 3.0-liter works entirely alone, ditching the electric motor for razor-sharp throttle response.
- Aero Overkill: Massive wings and ducts produce 1,360 pounds of downforce at 155 mph to keep you glued to the pavement.
- Racing Pedigree: Developed alongside the 2027 Challenge Evo, this is a track-day weapon with a license plate.

Don’t expect this car to hold your hand like the tech-heavy 296 Speciale. This is a "disruption of balance" that demands your full attention and rewards you with a 32% torque boost over the GTB.

At roughly $307,000, it’s the ultimate flex for anyone who misses the days when driving required actual effort and zero plug-in cables. Just try not to curb those expensive rims on the way to pick up your morning espresso.
