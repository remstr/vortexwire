Toyota decided that "sensible" was a boring brand identity for the 2026 SEMA Show. Instead of leaning into fuel economy, they’ve debuted five Woodland concepts that look ready to jump a curb—or a small boulder.

The lineup includes a RAV4, Grand Highlander, Sienna, bZ electric crossover, and Crown Signia. Every single one has been doused in "Horizon Blue," a 1960s throwback shade that makes these modern haulers look like they belong in a vintage postcard.

Toyota’s CALTY Design team gave each vehicle a rugged starter pack:
- Custom lift kits paired with beefy all-terrain tires.
- Oversized fender flares and satin-black wheels.
- Heavy-duty roof racks for the gear you probably won't actually buy.

The standout is arguably the Sienna Woodland Concept. It is a minivan that desperately wants to be a Jeep, proving that hauling kids to soccer and light overlanding aren't mutually exclusive.

Even the all-electric bZ gets the treatment. It’s a clear signal that Toyota doesn't think EVs should be restricted to the paved parking lots of your local organic grocer.

A quick reality check for the weekend warriors: don't try this at home just yet. These are "special project prototypes," meaning these specific mods might void your warranty or render your car illegal for street use.

For now, they are just expensive, beautiful eye candy. It’s a vibe, even if it’s one that mostly lives on the showroom floor.
