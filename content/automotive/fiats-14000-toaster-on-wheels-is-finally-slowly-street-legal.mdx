Fiat’s Topolino is finally ditching the "glorified golf cart" label for something slightly more respectable. For the 2027 model year, the Italian brand is including a free Low-Speed Vehicle (LSV) conversion kit that makes this adorable EV officially street-legal on American soil.

Don’t expect to win any drag races, or even keep up with a fit cyclist. The kit boosts the top speed from a leisurely 19 mph to a blistering 25 mph, which legally permits you to roam roads with speed limits of 35 mph or less. 

- The tiny 5.0-kWh battery offers 46 miles of range, enough to get you to the coffee shop and back without a range-anxiety-induced panic attack.
- A new "Open Air" body style offers a soft-top roof and optional doors for those who enjoy the "living on the edge" aesthetic.
- Interior perks include a full-color digital cluster and magnetic Bluetooth speakers you can rip out for beach-side jams.

At $13,995, you’re essentially buying an 8-horsepower Italian appliance that looks like it belongs in a Pixar movie. It even comes in "Corallo Orange" and "Luna Greige" for those who find traditional car colors a bit too aggressive. 

Fiat is even offering free home delivery within 50 miles of a dealer. That's a necessary perk, considering driving this home on a suburban highway would be a literal suicide mission. It’s the ultimate "main character" car for people who find parallel parking to be their greatest life adversary.
