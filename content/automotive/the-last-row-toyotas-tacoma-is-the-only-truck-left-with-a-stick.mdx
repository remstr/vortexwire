The manual gearbox is currently on life support, and the pickup truck segment is about to pull the plug. If you’re the type of person who refuses to let a computer decide when to shift, your list of options has officially dwindled to a single name: the Toyota Tacoma.

While rumors suggested Jeep might bring back the manual Gladiator for 2027, those whispers haven't materialized into actual hardware. For now, Toyota is the only manufacturer keeping the three-pedal dream alive, even if only about one percent of buyers actually opt for it.

- The "Save the Manuals" starter pack: You’ll need to spec a non-hybrid SR, TRD Sport, or TRD Off-Road trim.
- The Fine Print: The stick is strictly paired with the five-foot bed, double-cab, and four-wheel drive configuration.
- Power Stats: You’re looking at 270 horses and 310 lb-ft of torque, with a respectable 6,400-pound towing capacity.

Starting under $40,000, it’s a relatively affordable way to ensure nobody under the age of 25 can successfully valet your vehicle. In an era of homogenized automatics, this setup is a guaranteed future collector's item for the Bring a Trailer crowd.

If you want to row your own gears, grab one before Toyota realizes they’re building a truck for a demographic that barely exists. Your left leg will thank you, even if your heavy-traffic commute doesn't.
