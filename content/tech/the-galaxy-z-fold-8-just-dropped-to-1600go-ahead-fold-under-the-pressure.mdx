Samsung is making it $300 easier to join the foldable elite before the Black Friday chaos officially descends. The Galaxy Z Fold 8 is currently sitting at $1,599.99 at Amazon and Best Buy, which is just $50 shy of its all-time lowest price.

If you’ve been eyeing that passport-style frame but couldn’t justify a "used car" price tag, this is your green light. The shorter, wider aspect ratio makes the device feel more like a pocket-sized iPad than a bulky candy bar, which is perfect for binge-watching or pretending to work.

- Snag a $300 discount on the unlocked model at major retailers.
- Double your storage to 512GB for an extra $200 if you’re a total digital hoarder.
- The 4,800mAh battery is a beast, easily surviving a full day of use with plenty of juice to spare.

You aren’t getting the "Ultra" bells and whistles here, so keep your expectations in check. You’ll have to survive without a telephoto lens, and the IP48 rating means this device is definitely not a fan of beach sand or accidental latte baths.

Still, for a phone that hit shelves only a few months ago, it's a massive win for anyone who values screen real estate over beach-proof durability. If you’re still feeling spendy, the early sales also extend to the new Beats Pill speaker and Lego Star Wars droids—because your desk is looking a little too professional.
