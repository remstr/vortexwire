Meta just gave its Muse AI a functional makeover, and for once, it’s not just another chatbot trying to write your cousin's wedding toast. The new “spaces” feature allows users to whip up customized, app-like dashboards directly within the interface without touching a single line of code.

Think of it as a DIY digital Lego set for your daily life. You can describe a shopping hub, a school tracker, or a niche trivia game, and Muse will manifest it into existence.

- **Automatic Updates:** Connect a space to your apps, and it’ll track price drops or date changes in the background while you sleep.
- **Scheduling:** Pin your work planner to the sidebar on Monday and swap it for a weekend itinerary by Friday night.
- **Customization:** Tweak the layout or functionality just by asking the AI to move things around or add notes.

The rollout includes a "specialized" Muse Wonder model designed to handle these more complex, persistent tasks. Meta is also hinting at future collaborative spaces, presumably so you can share your obsessive vacation spreadsheet with the friends you're about to annoy.

It’s a blatant move to keep you inside the Meta ecosystem longer, but if it replaces five different half-baked productivity apps, we might actually let them. Mark Zuckerberg is betting you'd rather talk to a robot than navigate your own messy home screen.
