We all think we’re geniuses for hiding under a patio umbrella in 90-degree heat. But UV radiation doesn’t care about your outdoor furniture or your "cool" fan setup. 

One Los Angeles local learned this the hard way while testing the Gem, a $249 UV-tracking necklace that looks like jewelry but acts like a tiny, nagging scientist. While she thought she was safe in the shade at a party, the device pinged her with a high-exposure alert. 

Turns out, radiation reflects off surfaces like gossip at a brunch table. You aren't just getting hit from above; you're getting hit from the ground, the walls, and the neighbor’s pool.

- Vanity Check: UVA causes premature aging, turning your skin into a vintage leather sofa before its time.
- Burn Notice: UVB is the culprit behind sunburns, but it’s also how your body manufactures Vitamin D.
- Med Alert: Realizing high UV was the problem led the author to discover her medications were actually causing heat sensitivity.

What started as a quest to stay youthful turned into a necessary health intervention. The Gem didn’t provide a medical diagnosis, but it did provide the data needed to stop ignoring chronic dizzy spells. 

If you’re living in a sun-soaked hellscape, your melanin levels aren’t a "get out of jail free" card. Sometimes it takes a piece of smart jewelry to remind you that the sun always finds a way.
