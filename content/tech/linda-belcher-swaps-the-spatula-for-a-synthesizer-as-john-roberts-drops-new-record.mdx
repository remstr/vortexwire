John Roberts, the vocal powerhouse behind Linda Belcher’s iconic "Alright!", is officially hanging up the apron—at least long enough to hit the dance floor. While Bob’s Burgers cruises through its sixteenth season, Roberts has been busy channeling his inner disco diva into something much more personal.

His new album, I Am, hit streaming platforms last Friday, and it’s a neon-soaked love letter to the NYC queer nightlife that raised him. It’s a sonic cocktail of 80s synth-pop, 90s house, and a dash of 00s rave energy designed for both sweaty clubs and quiet existential crises.

- Creative Pivot: Roberts is trading animated family dynamics for heavy-hitting, genre-bending dance tracks.
- Deep Cuts: Despite the high-energy beats, the record tackles heavy themes like mortality and the grief of losing his mother.
- Visual Vibe: The music video for “Complicated” is a campy homage to Scorpio Rising and the gritty aesthetic of cult cinema.

Roberts admits he’s mostly just relieved to finish a project without overthinking it into a state of paralysis. He’s a guy who still buys Madonna on vinyl and finds his "happy place" in grainy YouTube clips of campy TV performances.

It’s a gutsy move for a comedian whose voice is burned into the collective consciousness of every millennial. If you’ve ever wanted to process your grief while doing a rhythmic two-step, Linda Belcher has officially provided the soundtrack. Roberts proves that even when life gets heavy, you can always find the beat.
