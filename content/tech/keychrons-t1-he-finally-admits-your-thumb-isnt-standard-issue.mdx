Keychron is clearly tired of the "one-size-fits-most" lie that’s been haunting ergonomic peripherals for decades. Their new T1 HE trackball mouse acknowledges a radical truth: human hands are weirdly different.

Unlike its slim predecessor, the T1 HE adopts a classic side-ball layout. However, it hides a clever party trick on its underside that actually solves the ergonomic struggle.

- **The Flex:** Two dials on the bottom allow you to fine-tune the trackball's horizontal and vertical positioning.
- **The Reach:** While the adjustments are only a few millimeters, it’s enough to ensure the 34mm ball sits squarely under your thumb instead of forcing a yoga stretch.
- **The Specs:** You’re looking at a 100-2,400 DPI range, six remappable buttons, and triple-mode connectivity (Bluetooth, 2.4GHz, and USB-C).
- **The Juice:** The 600mAh battery delivers about 106 hours over Bluetooth, or 98 hours if you prefer the 2.4GHz dongle.

At $59.99, it’s priced aggressively enough to make your aging office mouse look a bit prehistoric. It comes in black, white, or a translucent finish for the tech-voyeurs who like to see the internal guts.

Preorders are live now on Keychron's site, with shipping expected to kick off in early December. If you’re tired of your hardware dictating your grip, this might be the thumb-saver you’ve been waiting for.
