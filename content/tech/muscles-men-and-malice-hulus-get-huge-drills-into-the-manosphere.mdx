If you thought "looksmaxxing" was just a cringe TikTok trend involving jawline exercises, Hulu’s new docuseries Get Huge: Looksmaxxing to Death is here to ruin your afternoon. Director Stacey Lee dives into the unsettling world of Anthony “Tony Huge” Hughes, a former bankruptcy lawyer turned steroid evangelist living a neon-soaked fever dream in Thailand.

- The Lead: Tony Huge, a man who traded his law license for a syringe and an influencer empire built on unapproved performance-enhancers.
- The Hook: How "chemical biohacking" and literal bone-breaking became the go-to hobbies for the internet's most impressionable men.
- The Damage: A deep dive into the radicalization of a generation convinced that happiness is just one more dangerous cycle away.

The series tracks how Hughes and his disciples preach a gospel of aesthetic perfection that’s essentially a slow-motion suicide pact. From his base in Pattaya, Hughes showcases a life of nonstop partying and muscle-swelling injections, presenting himself as a digital king with a harem of women.

Lee captures the grim reality behind the "alpha" filters: a cycle of FDA investigations and a desperate need for power over others. It turns out that when you build your entire personality on being a "peak specimen," the only thing left to do is crack under the weight of your own ego. These men aren't just building bigger bodies; they're constructing their own psychological prisons in 4K resolution.
