Arion Kurtaj, the teenager who famously turned Rockstar Games’ secret stash into public property, just received a seven-year sentence—and then promptly walked out the front door. After leaking 90 clips of Grand Theft Auto VI back in 2022, the hacker found himself in a legal shootout that concluded with a release on license.

The 20-year-old wasn't exactly a one-hit wonder, having also poked around the internals of Uber and Revolut during his spree. While the judge acknowledged his "obvious talent," she made sure his digital social life would be as barren as a desert in Blaine County for the foreseeable future.

- Sentenced to seven years, but released on license following time spent in psychiatric care and custody.
- Subject to a five-year Serious Crime Prevention Order to keep his hardware in check.
- Strictly banned from using Telegram or Discord, which is effectively a social death sentence for a modern cyber-prodigy.

The timing of his release is poetic, given that Rockstar finally unleashed GTA VI last week to the kind of reviews that make critics weep. The studio pocketed a casual $3.79 billion in just 24 hours, proving that even a massive pre-release leak couldn't derail the most anticipated launch in history.

Kurtaj has been warned to find a productive outlet for his skills before he ends up back in the system. For now, he’ll have to settle for watching the GTA VI hype from the sidelines—and without any chatroom bragging rights.
