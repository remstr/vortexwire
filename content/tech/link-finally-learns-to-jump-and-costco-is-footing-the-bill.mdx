Nintendo has finally addressed the most glaring oversight of 1998: Link can now jump whenever he damn well pleases. This reimagined Ocarina of Time for Switch 2 isn’t just a resolution bump; it’s a mechanical overhaul of the game that defined your childhood.

If you’re already paying for a Costco membership to buy 48-packs of toilet paper, there’s a new perk in the digital aisle. Members can currently snag the remaster for $54.99, shaving five bucks off the standard $60 sticker price.

Sure, a five-dollar discount doesn’t seem like a windfall, but let’s apply some "gamer math." That saving covers nearly 8% of your annual membership fee—or more importantly, three-and-a-third legendary hot dog combos.

- Visuals feature a blend of realistic environments and "puppet-like" character designs.
- Quality-of-life updates make the clunky N64 mechanics feel actually modern.
- Manual jumping is a total game-changer for Hyrulean platforming.

Reviewers note that while the scenery looks gorgeous, the soul of the 64-bit classic remains intact. It’s the same Hyrule you remember, just without the headache of automated ledge-hopping or muddy textures.

Grab the deal online and use the savings to fund your next stamina potion—or a massive jar of mayonnaise. Hyrule is waiting, and for once, the barrier to entry is slightly cheaper.
