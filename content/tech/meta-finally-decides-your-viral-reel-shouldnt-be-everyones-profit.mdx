Instagram is tired of being the internet’s favorite thrift store for stolen content. The platform is rolling out a new "content protection" feature, finally giving creators a way to police the digital shoplifters who build entire followings off other people's Reels.

For those enrolled, the tool scans Facebook and Instagram for visual matches of your original work. If someone rips your video, you get a notification and the power to choose their fate.

- **The Kill Switch:** You can hide the offending video from everyone on both platforms.
- **The Credit Grab:** Feeling generous? Add a credit link to the copycat’s post to redirect the clout back to the source.
- **The Tracking:** Just sit back and watch how many views your stolen work is racking up before you pull the plug.

It’s not just about lazy reposts. Instagram is also hunting down AI-generated clones that use your original audio but swap in deepfake visuals. This "voice-matching" tech aims to stop those weird AI accounts that thrive on stealing your vibe while replacing your face.

- All new posts are protected automatically once you enroll.
- Older videos can be manually added to the vault.
- An "allow list" ensures your collaborators don’t get hit with an accidental takedown.

The feature is currently testing with a small group of creators. If you aren’t one of them yet, keep a close eye on your "Original Audio" tab—the vultures are still hungry.
