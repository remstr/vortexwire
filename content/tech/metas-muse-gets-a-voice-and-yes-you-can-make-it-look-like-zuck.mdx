Meta is tired of you just typing your feelings into a void. Mark Zuckerberg just announced that Muse, Meta’s AI agent, is officially entering its vocal era with real-time voice conversations and "expressive" avatars.

The update allows the AI to animate its face in sync with its speech. It’s a bit like FaceTime, if your friend was an algorithm that never sleeps and occasionally experiences a three-second lag while "thinking."

Users can browse through various voice profiles or ask the AI to change its tone on the fly. Want it to sound more authoritative? Done. Want it to sound like it’s judging your late-night Amazon orders? Probably also doable.

- **Real-Time Chatter:** Voice responses are snappy, though the AI still pauses to "search" for answers, much like a human pretending they knew the trivia answer all along.
- **The Uncanny Valley:** You can actually set the avatar to look like Mark Zuckerberg himself. It’s a great way to remember who’s hosting the party, but seeing a digital Zuck facsimile mouth your grocery list is objectively haunting.
- **Coming Soon:** The feature is live now for US app users. It’s hitting Meta’s AI glasses and the upcoming Muse Charm wearable later this year.

Whether you want a digital companion or just a more interactive way to set a timer, Muse is ready to talk. Just try not to look the Zuckerberg avatar directly in the pixels.
