WhatsApp is finally solving the “I have a great idea at 2 AM but don't want to get blocked” dilemma. Meta’s crown jewel is rolling out a message scheduling feature, allowing its 3 billion users to draft texts, photos, or videos and time-stamp them for the future.

It’s a tool other platforms have boasted for years, but WhatsApp’s late arrival is a classic "better late than never" moment. You can now queue up everything from birthday wishes to passive-aggressive rent reminders anywhere from ten minutes to two weeks in advance.

The mechanics are dead simple:
- Long-press the send button to pull up the scheduling menu.
- Pick your date and time, then let Meta’s servers do the heavy lifting.
- The message sends even if your phone is dead or you’re currently off-grid.

Crucially, these time-capsule texts remain end-to-end encrypted. Not even Mark Zuckerberg can see your scheduled groveling for a deadline extension or that midnight confession you'll probably regret by breakfast.

The recipient won’t see a "scheduled" tag, so you can successfully gaslight your friends into thinking you’re a functional human who actually wakes up at 6 AM. You can also view, edit, or delete pending messages before they fire off, saving you from those inevitable "what was I thinking?" moments.

The update is rolling out globally over the next few weeks. Use it wisely, or at least use it to make your boss think you’re working through the weekend.
