Google’s search page has been the Marie Kondo of the internet for decades—just a vast white void and a single, lonely bar. That minimalist dream is officially over as the company wedges a persistent AI chat sidebar onto your desktop.

Vice President of Product Robby Stein recently revealed the new "AI Mode" sidebar, a collapsible gray tab that lets you dive back into your previous digital rabbit holes without re-typing everything. It’s a formal admission that our queries are no longer simple "one and done" searches.

Here is what the sidecar setup actually brings to your browser:
- Pick up exactly where you left off in previous AI-powered conversations.
- Launch new threads or search through your chaotic history of past queries.
- Connect directly to Gemini Notebooks for those rare moments when you’re actually being productive.

This rollout confirms the vibe shift at Mountain View: Google is tired of being a simple librarian and wants to be your full-time research assistant. The classic search box already evolved to handle longer queries and AI-powered autocomplete, but the sidebar is where the actual "dialogue" lives.

If you’re seeing a new gray bar creeping onto the left side of your screen, don't call tech support. It’s just Google reminding you that your search history is now a living, breathing conversation that—for better or worse—never actually ends.
