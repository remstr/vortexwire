Apple spent the summer hiking prices like it was trying to fund a private island, but the tech giants have finally blinked. If you’ve been staring at a digital cart for months waiting for the M5 MacBook Air to stop being aggressively expensive, your patience just paid off.

The 13-inch base model—packing the M5 chip, 16GB of RAM, and a 512GB SSD—is currently sitting at $1,099.99. It’s the first time we’ve seen this $200 discount since the mid-summer sticker shock, and frankly, it’s a welcome return to reality.

- **The 13-inch Air:** Now $1,099.99 at Amazon, Best Buy, and B&H.
- **The 15-inch Air:** Down to $1,299.99, offering a bigger screen and a six-speaker array that actually sounds decent.
- **The Specs:** Both sales cover the 16GB/512GB configuration across all color options.

While the "MacBook Neo" might tempt those looking for the absolute lowest price point, the Air remains the grown-up choice here. You’re getting double the storage, a backlit keyboard that doesn’t feel like mush, and a CPU that won't cry when you open more than two spreadsheets.

Deals this deep have been rarer than a coherent movie plot lately. Amazon and Best Buy are leading the charge, so if you’re looking to upgrade before the holiday rush turns every shipping warehouse into a scene from Mad Max, this is your window.
