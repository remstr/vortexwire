Nvidia makes $8,300 in pure profit every single second. By the time you finish this sentence, they’ve likely cleared enough to buy a mid-range Tesla without checking the bank balance.

Following Jensen Huang’s abrupt exit amidst a flurry of scandals, standard corporate logic suggests the company is in trouble. But when you’re minting $700 million a day, "trouble" is a very comfortable place to be.

The directive for the next captain is simple: don’t touch a single thing. While the crypto craze of yesteryear felt massive, it’s a mere rounding error compared to the current AI-fueled data center explosion. 

- **Quarterly Profits:** Surged from a $1.2 billion "peak" during the GPU shortage to a staggering $65 billion today.
- **Employee Perks:** The firm could hand every one of its 42,000 staffers a $1 million bonus every three months and still have change for the vending machine.
- **Market Cap:** Even after Jensen effectively incinerated a trillion dollars in valuation recently, the company still sits at a "modest" $3.6 trillion.

Rivals like Intel, Qualcomm, and AMD aren’t really competition anymore. They’re just background noise in a world where Nvidia owns the silicon keys to the kingdom.

The new leadership isn’t here to "disrupt" or "pivot" the business model. They just want to ensure the most profitable boat in human history doesn't hit a single wave.
