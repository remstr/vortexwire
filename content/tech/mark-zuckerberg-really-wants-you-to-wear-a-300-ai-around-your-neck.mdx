Meta is finally putting a price tag on the Muse Charm, and it’s a cool $300 for the privilege of talking to your chest. Mark Zuckerberg’s latest hardware gamble is reportedly hitting shelves this December, aiming to be the most expensive stocking stuffer of the year.

Think of it as a standalone portal for Meta’s Muse AI agent, complete with a tiny screen and a fingerprint sensor. You tap it, you talk to it, and it responds with real-time voice and avatar tech—all without the "exhausting" effort of reaching into your pocket for a smartphone.

To make that $300 pill easier to swallow, the device reportedly includes a free 12-month 5G data plan from Verizon. It’s a classic "first taste is free" strategy to get you hooked on a gadget that exists solely because Meta wants to own the hardware you interact with every day.

The essentials:
- Price: $300 (or about three months of actual groceries).
- ETA: This December, just in time for the holiday gift-giving rush.
- Collateral Damage: Meta’s actual smartwatch has been pushed to 2027 to prioritize this launch.

Meta is leaning hard into the "AI everywhere" era, even if it means you look like you’re wearing a high-tech garage door opener. If $300 feels steep for a digital locket, the company has open-sourced the code so you can build your own version at home. 

Just don't expect Verizon to pay for your data plan if you go the DIY route and glue a Raspberry Pi to your shirt.
