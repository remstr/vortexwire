Happy Election Day. If your bank account felt a sharp, stabbing pain while filling up your tank this morning, you certainly aren't alone.

National gas prices are currently sitting at a record $4.12 a gallon, with diesel hovering at a nauseating $6.28. It is officially the most expensive commute to a polling station in American history.

For years, the GOP focused on "drill, baby, drill" while treating electric vehicles like a personal affront to the American way of life. They successfully dismantled the $7,500 federal tax credit and choked off charging infrastructure, effectively tethering voters to the pump indefinitely.

- Gas prices are at an all-time Election Day high, while the Strategic Petroleum Reserve hits a 44-year low.
- The $7,500 "point of sale" tax credit is gone, leaving the average EV transaction price well over $50,000.
- Surging geopolitical tensions have sent crude prices soaring, but the electric "escape hatch" has been welded shut by policy.

Now that foreign conflicts are spiking oil prices, that "culture war" against batteries feels a lot more like a hostage situation. Drivers who once mocked EVs are now haunting dealerships, desperate for any hybrid that doesn't require a second mortgage to refuel.

It turns out you can’t run a modern economy solely on nostalgia and fossil fuels. When the bill comes due at the pump, "owning the libs" doesn't exactly help you cover the cost of a full tank.
