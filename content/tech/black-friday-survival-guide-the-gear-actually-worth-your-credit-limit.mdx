Black Friday has officially mutated from a mall-based bloodsport into a digital siege on your bank account. While "RAMageddon" has kept some tech prices stubbornly high this year, the floodgates are finally open on gear that actually matters.

We aren't just looking for lower numbers; we're looking for survival tools for the modern age. If you’ve been waiting for prices to return to their pre-inflation glory, this is your window before inventory evaporates.

- Apple AirPods Pro 3: They’ve added heart rate tracking and live translation, making them the overachievers of the earbud world.
- Disney Plus/Hulu Bundle: At $4.99 a month for a year, you’re getting a massive streaming catalog for the price of one mediocre airport sandwich.
- Anker 100W Travel Adapter: A three-port USB-C beast with an LCD display that works in 200 countries—perfect for your next escape.
- Xteink X4: A tiny 4.3-inch E Ink reader that snaps onto MagSafe phones because apparently, a standard Kindle is now too "bulky."
- Nintendo Switch 2: The hype is finally manifesting with a 7.9-inch display and magnetic Joy-Cons for your mobile gaming fix.

Don't expect these to linger until Cyber Monday; inventory moves fast when everyone is stress-shopping. Whether you’re upgrading your audio or finally buying that pocket-sized library, the time to pull the trigger is now.
