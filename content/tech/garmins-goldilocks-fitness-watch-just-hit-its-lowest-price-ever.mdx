Most fitness trackers are either oversized wrist-bricks that look like you're wearing a Pip-Boy or glorified pedometers that stop working if you look at them wrong. The Garmin Forerunner 265S has spent years sitting comfortably in the middle, marred only by a retail price that usually makes buyers flinch.

That barrier just evaporated. Amazon and Best Buy have slashed $150 off the MSRP, bringing this OLED standout down to $299.99—its most aggressive price of the year.

This isn’t a clearance fire sale for an obsolete paperweight. It’s the "Goldilocks" of the Garmin lineup, offering the perfect balance of high-end metrics and everyday wearability.

The "S" stands for small, making it the go-to for runners who don’t want a 50mm sundial weighing down their pace. It’s arguably better than the standard 265, boasting slightly more endurance despite its more compact 42mm frame.

- Vibrant OLED touchscreen that remains legible even under the harsh midday sun.
- Long-term firmware support that keeps Garmin’s older hardware feeling brand new.
- Deep fitness analytics that help you train smarter without needing a PhD.

While the tech world obsesses over the next shiny object, the 265S remains the sweet spot for anyone who wants serious data without a mortgage-sized investment. Snag it before the holiday inventory dries up and you’re forced back into the arms of a $500 alternative you don’t actually need.
