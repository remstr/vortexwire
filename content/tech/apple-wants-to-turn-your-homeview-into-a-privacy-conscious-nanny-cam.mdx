Apple’s HomeView might soon be doing more than just sitting on your kitchen counter looking expensive. Reddit sleuths digging through the HomeView 27.2 code have uncovered a "Secure Video" mode that essentially turns your smart hub into a security guard.

If the strings are to be believed, you’ll soon get motion detection, live streaming, and recording without having to buy a separate Nest cam. It’s a major win for anyone who wondered why Apple didn't include this functionality on day one.

Of course, because this is Apple, the surveillance comes with a heavy dose of privacy safeguards. The setup page explicitly mentions HomeKit Secure Video integration, ensuring your footage doesn't wander off into the wrong hands.

- Indicator lights and sounds will alert you whenever someone is peeking through the lens.
- Notifications will blast your phone the second any security settings are tweaked.
- Recording triggers can be customized based on whether you're home, away, or currently unconscious in bed.

The feeds are strictly for "home members" only, keeping the footage away from everyone except your inner circle. Apple hasn't officially confirmed the update yet, likely because they’re busy polishing the privacy UI until it sparkles.

While we wait for the official drop, here is the breakdown of what to expect:
- Full HomeKit Secure Video integration.
- Customizable triggers for home, away, and sleep modes.
- Audible and visual alerts for active recording or live viewing.
