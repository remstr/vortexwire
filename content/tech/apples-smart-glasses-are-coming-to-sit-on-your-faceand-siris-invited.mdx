Apple is finally preparing to move its tech from your pocket to your pupils. According to the latest leaks, the company’s long-rumored smart glasses—codenamed N50—are set for a grand reveal at next June’s WWDC.

Don’t expect a Minority Report-style heads-up display just yet; these are "smart," not augmented. Think of them as a very stylish home for a newly revamped, actually-competent Siri.

- The Guts: A pared-down version of visionOS powered by a chip based on older smartwatch tech.
- The Juice: A "Tic-Tac" sized MagSafe charger that can apparently snap on while you’re still wearing the frames.
- The Vibe: High-end audio for FaceTime and music, with a major focus on privacy and aesthetic.

The hardware relies heavily on your iPhone for the heavy lifting, though built-in Wi-Fi means you won't be totally tethered for basic audio tasks. It’s a calculated gamble on style over complex optics, aiming to succeed where the bulky, goggle-heavy Vision Pro stumbled.

Apple is betting that by the time these ship late next year, Siri will be smart enough to run your life via voice command. If you can stomach a tiny magnetic charger stuck to your temple, your face is about to become the ultimate peripheral.
