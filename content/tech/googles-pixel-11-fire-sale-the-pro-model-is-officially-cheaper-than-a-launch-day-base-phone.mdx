Google is apparently tired of waiting for Black Friday, slashing prices on the Pixel 11 lineup by up to 27 percent through November 8. If you’ve been eyeing the shiny new Pro model but balked at the four-digit price tag, your patience just paid a $350 dividend.

The standout here is the Pixel 11 Pro, now sitting at $799.99—comfortably beating its Prime Day price and effectively making the base model look like a bad investment. You're getting the 256GB storage bump and 12GB of RAM for less than what the standard version cost at launch.

- Pixel 11 Pro: $799.99 (Save $300)
- Pixel 11: $699.99 (Save $200)
- High-value trade-ins: Older Pixels are currently fetching up to $530 in credit.

While the base Pixel 11 is a great entry point for software tricks like Magic Capture and Proactive Assistance, the Pro is the clear winner here. For a relatively small jump in price, you get the superior camera array and the "HiLight" features you’ll probably use once and then brag about forever.

Even the massive Pixel 11 Pro XL is seeing deep cuts for those who prioritize battery life over pocket comfort. Don’t expect these numbers to drop further come late November; Google has played its best hand early, likely to steal the spotlight from Apple’s recent price hikes.
