Apple just ushered in the touchscreen MacBook Pro era, and apparently, it brought a pricing strategy designed to make your credit card weep. The base model starts at $2,899, already a tough pill to swallow in today's economy. But if you're aiming for the ultimate 16-inch beast with every conceivable upgrade, prepare to shell out a staggering $14,549. That's not a typo.

For perspective, the previous top-tier 16-inch MacBook Pro maxed out at $10,149. Apple has effectively raised the price ceiling by a jaw-dropping $4,400. Much of that eye-watering cost comes from the powerful M5 Max chip, boasting an 18-core CPU and 40-core GPU, along with the tech industry’s insatiable hunger for AI hardware.

Curious where your life savings might go?
- A 4TB SSD upgrade tacks on $1,000.
- Need 8TB? That'll be an additional $3,000.
- For a colossal 16TB, you're looking at a cool $7,000.
- Bumping to 128GB of memory will cost you another $2,000.
- Even nano-texture glass demands an extra $150.

Absurd pricing aside, this release signals a major strategic shift for Apple. After nearly a decade and a somewhat awkward flirtation with the Touch Bar, Cupertino is finally embracing the full touchscreen experience on its flagship laptops. Preorders kick off October 30, with availability starting November 6. Time to check under the couch cushions.
