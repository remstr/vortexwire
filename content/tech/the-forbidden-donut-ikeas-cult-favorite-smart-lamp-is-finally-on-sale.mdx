Ikea’s Varmblixt lamp looks less like a basic light fixture and more like a high-end prop from a retro-futuristic synth-pop music video. It’s a literal glowing glass donut, and right now, it’s $20 cheaper than usual at the Swedish furniture giant.

Through November 11, you can snag this frosted ring of light for $79.99. It’s the perfect pickup for anyone who wants their studio apartment to feel less like a cluttered cave and more like a boutique hotel in downtown Stockholm.

Don’t expect it to do the heavy lifting in a pitch-black room, though. At a modest 180 lumens, this isn't the lamp you use to perform surgery or hunt for a lost contact lens; it’s pure aesthetic. Think of it as a "mood lamp" designed to sit on a side table or hang on a wall like a piece of minimalist gallery art.

- **Color Theory:** The physical remote cycles through 12 presets, but Matter support means you can unlock hundreds of custom hues via Apple Home, Google Home, or Alexa.
- **Versatile AF:** It’s designed to be wall-mounted with a single screw or simply rested on any flat surface.
- **Smart Smarts:** Sync it with Ikea’s Dirigera hub to automate your lighting schedules or mimic natural daylight patterns throughout the afternoon.

If you missed the initial hype train for these glowing orange pastries, now is the time to bite. It’s a small price to pay for a smart home upgrade that actually looks like it belongs in this century.
