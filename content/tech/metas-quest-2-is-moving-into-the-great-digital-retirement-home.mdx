The Quest 2, that strapped-on gateway to 2020’s social isolation, finally has an expiration date. Meta announced today that its most successful headset—and its awkward, expensive sibling, the Quest Pro—will stop receiving OS updates in March 2027.

The final curtain call lands with version 213. After that, you’ll get security patches and "critical" bug fixes through the end of 2027, but don't expect any shiny new features to drop on your aging face-computer.

- The Quest 3 and 3S are officially the new "baseline" for Meta’s VR ecosystem.
- New VR glasses are slated for a Spring 2027 release, conveniently as the Quest 2 enters its sunset phase.
- Developers can keep submitting apps for the legacy hardware until December 2027.

It’s a classic pivot from the Zuckerberg playbook: making the old stuff feel like a paperweight just as the next big thing arrives. The Quest 2 had a hell of a run, but Meta is done pretending that 2020 mobile chips can keep up with its mixed-reality future.

If you’re still rocking the Quest Pro, this news probably hits a little harder. Launched in 2022 and killed off in 2024, it’s essentially a $1,500 paperweight-in-waiting that proved to be a very expensive beta test.

Time to start eyeing that Quest 3S, or prepare for your VR sessions to go retro way faster than you expected.
