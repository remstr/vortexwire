Whitney Peak is heading to Panem, but she’s bringing a pillow. The 23-year-old star of *Sunrise on the Reaping* has officially mastered the most vital survival skill in the "Hunger Games" universe—and no, it’s not archery. It’s the tactical nap.

Peak joins the franchise as Lenore Dove Baird, a member of District 12’s musical Covey and the girlfriend of a young, pre-boozy Haymitch Abernathy. While the 50th Games are full of trauma and tribute-on-tribute violence, Peak’s biggest personal hurdle was the grueling "hurry up and wait" schedule of a massive film set.

- Peak plays a key role in the prequel, set 24 years before Katniss Everdeen was a glimmer in the Capitol’s eye.
- Her character, Lenore, is part of the same nomadic community introduced in *The Ballad of Songbirds and Snakes*.
- The actress admits she can doze off for anywhere from five minutes to three hours, provided she isn't sitting upright.

The role wasn't just about catching Zs, though. Peak says the experience forced her to ditch her perfectionist streak and embrace the "high of facing fear."

Joining a multi-billion dollar franchise is enough to make anyone lose sleep, but Peak is choosing confidence over anxiety. She’s learned that showing up and giving it your all is more important than looking polished, even if you make a total fool of yourself in the process. Just don't ask her to nap in a chair—that’s where she draws the line.
