Japan is basically moving into the Palais des Festivals at this point. Following a high-profile stint as Country of Honor, the Japan IP Market is officially returning to the Cannes Film Market for a second round in May 2027.

TIFFCOM and Cannes are extending their partnership to keep the pipeline open between Tokyo and the French Riviera. They’ve locked in May 14–15 for the next edition, ensuring the world’s biggest film market stays fueled by Japanese creativity.

The 2026 inaugural run proved that international producers are absolutely starving for Japanese storytelling. Industry titans like Kadokawa, Shochiku, and Toei are showing up to pitch the kind of IP that makes global streamers salivate. 

The Vibe Check:
- Japanese rights holders get a direct line to global producers for adaptations and acquisitions.
- One-on-one meetings aim to turn cult-classic concepts into the next *One Piece* or *Shogun*.
- The initiative bridges the gap between Tokyo’s October buzz and the glitz of the European spring.

Even the heavy hitters are leaning in. Fujimura Tetsu, the executive producer behind the live-action *One Piece*, is a regular fixture in these discussions. It’s a calculated move to ensure Japanese content doesn't just stay local, but dominates every screen on the planet.

Expect plenty of espresso-fueled negotiations and, hopefully, fewer "lost in translation" adaptation deals. If the world is obsessed with Japanese IP right now, Cannes is the megaphone making sure everyone hears the pitch.
