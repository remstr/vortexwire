It turns out some dreams are actually quite expensive. A rare, handwritten draft of Martin Luther King Jr.’s iconic "I Have a Dream" speech is resurfacing from the archives and heading straight for the auction block. 

Christie’s is handling the sale this coming January. They’re estimating the document will fetch upwards of $10 million, proving that some scribbles are worth slightly more than your average grocery list.

- The draft offers a raw look at the revisions that shaped the 1963 March on Washington.
- It’s one of the few surviving physical links to a moment that fundamentally shifted American history.

Owning this isn't just about having a cool piece of paper to show off at dinner parties. It’s a literal blueprint of the civil rights movement, complete with the scratch-outs and edits of a man trying to change the world.

While most of us are struggling to find a working pen, MLK was busy drafting the moral compass of a nation. If you’ve got $10 million burning a hole in your pocket, this beats buying another boring tech startup or a depreciating luxury yacht.

- Christie’s expects the final price to shatter records for historical manuscripts.
- The auction marks a rare moment where a private collector can snag a foundational piece of the U.S. narrative.

History usually belongs in a museum, but in 2025, it apparently belongs to the highest bidder. Better start checking your couch cushions for spare change now.
