Hunter Schafer is currently failing upwards at a level we can only admire. The *Euphoria* breakout just landed a lead role in Prime Video’s *Blade Runner 2099*, despite the fact that acting was never actually on her career mood board.

She’s set to play Cora, a human caught in the middle of whatever high-concept existential dread the year 2099 has to offer. It’s a massive pivot for someone who originally moved to New York to become a fashion designer and illustrator.

Schafer has been refreshingly honest about her "accidental" career path. She stumbled into an open casting call for Jules Vaughn and effectively changed the DNA of Gen Z television, yet she still talks about her acting gigs like they are a very long, very high-budget detour.

- The Role: Cora, a human protagonist navigating a world increasingly crowded by replicants.
- The Pedigree: Ridley Scott is executive producing, with *Shining Girls* showrunner Silka Luisa at the helm.
- The Vibe: Expect more neon, more synthetic rain, and significantly more philosophical questioning of what "life" actually means.

While she might still be dreaming of the fashion runway, she’s currently stuck in our dystopian future. It’s a tough break, but at least the wardrobe department is guaranteed to be legendary.

If you're going to be a reluctant superstar, you might as well do it while running through a rain-slicked, neon-lit Los Angeles. We’ll take the accidental actress over the intentional influencer any day.
