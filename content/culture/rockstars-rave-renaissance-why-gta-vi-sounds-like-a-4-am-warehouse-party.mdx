If you were expecting GTA VI to be nothing but 80s power ballads and kitschy pop, it’s time to recalibrate your expectations. Rockstar Games is taking us back to the warehouse, channeling decades of rave obsession into the sonic landscape of Leonida.

The studio’s deep-seated love for the strobe-lit underground is finally taking center stage. We aren't just talking about a couple of techno tracks buried in a sub-menu; we're looking at a full-blown sonic overhaul featuring Justice and the tastemakers at CircoLoco.

- French electronic titans Justice are confirmed to bring their signature distorted disco to the official tracklist.
- CircoLoco, the global party legends, are curating a dedicated station to ensure your high-speed chases have a proper 4 A.M. Ibiza vibe.
- The soundtrack is designed to bridge the gap between classic house roots and the aggressive energy of modern techno.

This isn't some corporate pivot; it’s a homecoming for the developers. Rockstar’s leadership has been obsessed with club culture since the UK's 90s rave scene, and that DNA is finally being fully unleashed.

In GTA VI, the music is a deliberate vibe shift meant to capture the sweaty, kinetic energy of South Florida after the sun goes down. If the trailers are any indication, the airwaves will be just as chaotic as the streets themselves.

Whether you’re running from the law or just cruising the neon-soaked strips, the bass is going to be loud enough to rattle your digital teeth. Just don’t expect any generic radio fluff—this is a curated masterclass in electronic history.
