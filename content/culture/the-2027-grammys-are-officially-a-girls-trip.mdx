The Recording Academy just released the 69th Annual Grammy nominations, and the ballot looks like a highly curated "Girls' Night" playlist. If you aren't a woman with a genre-bending aesthetic or a massive vocal range, you’re basically just there for the appetizers this year.

British breakout Raye is the undisputed MVP, snagging 11 nominations for her signature blend of jazzy, throwback R&B. Trailing just behind is Rosalía with nine nods, proving that being "boundlessly eclectic" is a far better career strategy than playing it safe.

- Raye: 11 nods (The jazzy British invasion).
- Rosalía: 9 nods (Multilingual arena domination).
- Ella Langley: 7 nods (Ending country’s eight-year Album of the Year drought).
- Olivia Rodrigo & Phoebe Bridgers: 7 nods (Keeping the angst-industrial complex alive).

Ella Langley is the big story for Nashville, marking the first time a pure country artist has chased the top album prize in nearly a decade. She’s joined in the seven-nod club by Miranda Lambert, while Grammy darlings like Bruno Mars and Taylor Swift are still comfortably hovering in the top categories.

Even the broadcast is undergoing a mid-life crisis. After 50 years of playing nice with CBS, the Recording Academy is jumping ship to ABC and Disney+ for the February 7 ceremony in Los Angeles. 

Expect plenty of fashion chaos and at least one snub-related meltdown on social media. It’s going to be a long night, but at least the playlist is solid.
