Saturday Night Live finally remembered it was October, resurrecting the only Halloween mascot more confusing than a "sexy" insurance agent costume: David S. Pumpkins. Tom Hanks slipped back into that infamous pumpkin-print blazer this weekend during an episode hosted by *Obsession* star Inde Navarrette.

The premise remained blissfully stupid, featuring the usual trio of pelvic thrusts and absolutely zero context. But just as we were settling into the comfort of 2016-era nostalgia, James Austin Johnson’s Donald Trump decided to crash the haunted elevator.

- Hanks brings back the "Any questions?" energy, though we still don't have a single answer.
- Trump hijacks the sketch to pivot from spooky dancing to rambling campaign trail grievances.
- Those dancing skeletons remain the hardest-working, most rhythmic duo in late-night comedy.

It was a bizarre collision of two very different SNL eras. On one floor, you have a character who is proudly "his own thing," and on the other, a political impression that has basically become the show's permanent, unevictable roommate.

Seeing Hanks commit to the bit remains a genuine treat, even if the "trick" was just another reminder that we’re in the final sprint toward an election. Navarrette held her own as the host, but when a two-time Oscar winner is wiggling his hips in a suit covered in gourds, everyone else is effectively set dressing.

It was short, it was weird, and it was exactly the kind of chaos we needed. Now, can we please let the man put the wig back in storage for another four years?
