Julia Garner is done with being herself. The three-time Emmy winner has built a career on a specialized brand of alchemy, disappearing into accents and wigs until the original Garner is nowhere to be found. 

Her latest transformation? Diving into the wreckage of the crypto world for "The Altruists," a new series centered on the spectacular implosion of jailed founder Sam Bankman-Fried.

Garner describes her process as "living it," a phrase she admits sounds insufferably "actory." She wants to feel like no one is watching, a goal that’s hard to achieve when you’re a household name. 

Off-camera, Garner is less of a ghost and more of a "benevolent whirlwind." During a recent scouting mission at Zitomer’s—a posh Manhattan pharmacy—she was seen navigating a sea of bed jackets and furry Russian hats. 

She even attempted to aggressively gift high-end moisturizers to a reporter, proving her charm is just as indomitable as her acting chops.

- Next up: Garner trades Missouri trailers for crypto-chaos in the upcoming SBF-inspired project.
- The Method: She avoids "acting" once cameras roll, opting instead to simply exist within the moment.
- Retail Therapy: A lifelong regular at her neighborhood pharmacy, she still swears by blinged-out pill cases and something called "CelluMoist."

If her performance as a crypto-adjacent altruist is half as captivating as her skincare pitch, we’re in for a wild ride. Garner doesn't just play a part; she takes over the room.
