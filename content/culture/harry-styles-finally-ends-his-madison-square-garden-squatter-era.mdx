Harry Styles has finally packed his sequined suitcases and vacated Madison Square Garden. After a residency so long it practically qualified him for squatter’s rights, the British pop icon wrapped his massive New York takeover on Halloween night.

The finale, dubbed "Harryween," was less of a concert and more of a high-stakes religious experience for people who own too many hot glue guns. Styles spent the last few weeks effectively acting as the landlord of Midtown, proving New Yorkers will endure anything for a man in high-waisted trousers and a decent falsetto.

This residency wasn’t just a victory lap; it was a genuine endurance test for local feather boa suppliers and the MTA. Every subway car headed toward 34th Street recently has looked like a craft store exploded inside a disco.

- 30 total shows across the New York residency.
- A permanent banner raised to the MSG rafters, putting him in Billy Joel territory.
- Enough discarded glitter to pose a genuine threat to the city’s drainage system.

As Styles retreats to his next tour stop, the Garden’s janitorial staff is likely still excavating rhinestones from the HVAC vents. New York is significantly less sparkly today, but at least the traffic near Penn Station might finally return to its baseline level of standard misery.
