The 2019 breakout stars of *Booksmart* are finally putting the graduation gowns away and trading them for something much more terrifying: marriage-adjacent awkwardness. Skyler Gisondo, Beanie Feldstein, and Eduardo Franco are officially reuniting for *Focker In-Law*, a project that sounds like a spiritual successor to the Ben Stiller era of domestic cringe.

Since their high school hijinks five years ago, the trio has been busy colonizing every corner of the industry. Gisondo is currently prepping to play Jimmy Olsen in James Gunn’s *Superman*, while Feldstein has bounced from Broadway to portraying Monica Lewinsky. Franco, meanwhile, became the undisputed MVP of *Stranger Things* season four with his pitch-perfect pizza-delivery-guy energy.

- The film marks the first major collaboration for the three actors since Olivia Wilde’s directorial debut.
- Plot details are still mostly under wraps, but the title suggests a heavy dose of the *Meet the Parents* DNA.

Bringing this specific group back together feels like a rare win for fans of mid-budget comedies that actually have a soul. In an industry currently obsessed with capes and multiverses, seeing a cast with genuine comedic chemistry return to the fold is a breath of fresh air. 

Just don't expect the reunion to be any less chaotic than their legendary night out in Los Angeles. Let's hope they've learned how to handle overbearing parents better than they handled a fake ID.
