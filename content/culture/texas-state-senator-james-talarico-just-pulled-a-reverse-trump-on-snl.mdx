James Talarico is currently having a better week than most Democrats in the Lone Star State. The newly minted state senator-elect ditched the Austin humidity for the bright lights of Studio 8H this weekend, making a surprise cameo during the latest episode of Saturday Night Live.

While James Austin Johnson was busy delivering his signature, word-salad-heavy Trump impression, Talarico popped up to return the favor. Usually, it’s the former president sucking all the oxygen out of the room, but Talarico managed to steal the spotlight by giving the caricature a dose of his own interruption-heavy medicine.

- Talarico used the platform to pivot from the usual political gridlock to some surprisingly sharp comedic timing.
- The appearance follows his recent victory in the Texas Senate, cementing his status as a "one to watch" in a state that remains frustratingly red for his party.
- Host Druski and the SNL cast leaned into the absurdity, letting the real-life politician play the straight man to Johnson’s chaotic, rambling performance.

It’s a bold move for a guy who still has to face the Texas Legislature in a few months. Most politicians avoid live TV like the plague, but Talarico seems comfortable playing the foil to the most famous impersonation in late night. 

Whether this helps him pass bills in Austin is anyone's guess. However, he’s already won the battle for the most viral clip of the night, proving he’s at least ready for prime time.
